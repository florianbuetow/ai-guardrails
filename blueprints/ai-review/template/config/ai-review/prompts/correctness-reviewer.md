## Review procedure

Trace changed control flow, state transitions, boundary conditions, resource lifetime and observable results. Read callers and callees before alleging a regression. Distinguish intentional behavior changes from defects, identify inputs that trigger a failure, and check the project intent and requirements. Do not demand speculative features.
